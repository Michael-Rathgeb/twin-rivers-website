How often should you clean gutters in St. Louis?

Clean gutters at least twice a year in St. Louis: once in late fall after the leaves are down and once in spring. Oak trees are the reason spring matters. University of Missouri Extension notes that pin, red and white oaks keep many of their dried leaves into late winter, drop them a few at a time, and shed the last of them just before the buds open in spring. A November cleaning under oaks gets only part of the load. Maples add their winged seeds in spring.

What clogged gutters do to a house

A clogged gutter doesn't just overflow; it sends water to the places that are most expensive to fix:

  • Fascia and soffit rot: water held against the fascia board soaks it, and the rot spreads into the soffit and roof edge.
  • Roof edge leaks: water backing up can wick under the first course of shingles.
  • Stained and damaged siding under the overflow points.
  • Wet basements: water dumped at the wall instead of out of a downspout.
  • Sagging gutters: wet debris and winter ice add weight that pulls hangers loose.

Rotted fascia is a carpentry repair, which we handle along with repairing roof leaks at the roof edge, and stained walls sometimes end in siding replacement.

Do you still need to clean gutters with gutter guards?

Yes, less often. Guards keep leaves out of the trough, but they don't fix:

  • a gutter that's too small or has lost its pitch
  • seams and connectors that already leak
  • debris that collects on top of a screen or mesh
  • clogs at the downspout elbow from fine grit

That's why we inspect and repair before guards go on.

Gutter cleaning FAQs

How much does gutter cleaning cost?

It depends on how many feet of gutter you have, how high they are and how full they are. We inspect for free and give you the price in writing first.

When is the best time to clean gutters?

Late fall, once most leaves are down, and again in spring. Under oaks, spring is the one people skip and shouldn't. If you are booking holiday light installation, have the gutters cleaned first, since roofline lights clip to the gutter edge.

Can you clean gutters that already have guards?

Yes. We clear debris off the top, lift guards where needed to clean the trough, and flush the downspouts.

Will clean gutters stop my basement from getting wet?

They help, if the downspouts carry water away from the wall. If they empty at the foundation, add extensions or an underground drain.
