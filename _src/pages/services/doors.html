Prehung door or slab: what a replacement includes

A prehung door replaces the door, frame, hinges and sill as one unit; a slab replaces only the door panel. Prehung is the right call when the frame or sill is rotted, the door doesn't seal, or you're changing size or swing. A slab works only when the old frame is square and sound, and it has to be drilled and mortised to match the old hinges and lock.

  • Soft sill or rotted jamb bottom: prehung
  • Daylight around a closed door: prehung, with new weatherstripping
  • Frame sound, door cracked or dated: slab can work
  • Adding sidelights or a wider door: prehung and framing changes

Homes built before 1978 may have lead paint on the old trim. If replacing the door disturbs more than 20 sq ft of painted exterior surface, or 6 sq ft inside a room, the EPA's Renovation, Repair and Painting Rule requires a certified firm and lead-safe work practices.

Storm doors and your entry door warranty

Read the entry door's warranty before adding a storm door. A storm door in full sun can trap heat against the entry door, and several door manufacturers' published warranties limit or void coverage when a storm door is added, or when the door faces direct sun without an overhang. A south- or west-facing front door is where this matters most. We check the warranty terms for the door you choose before installing a storm door over it.

Choosing a front door color and finish

Pick a front door color that works with the roof, trim and siding, since the door is the one place a bold color is easy to change later. Fiberglass can be stained or painted; steel is painted. Dark colors absorb more heat, so a dark door in full sun is another reason to check the manufacturer's finish guidance. If you're redoing the outside, plan the door with siding replacement and replacement windows so the trim and colors match.

Door replacement FAQs

Is Home Depot or Lowe's better for doors?

Both sell a wide range of doors. The better choice depends on the installation: who does it, what the quote leaves out, and who stands behind the labor. Get one big-box quote and one local quote and compare them line by line.

How much will Home Depot or Lowe's charge to install a door?

Neither publishes a set price. Both quote after an in-home measure, and the price depends on the door and the condition of the opening.

How long does a door replacement take?

Once the door arrives, a standard prehung entry door is usually one visit. Framing changes or sidelights take longer.

Can you replace just the door and keep the frame?

Yes, if the frame is square and sound. That's a slab replacement, and it's checked at the consultation.
