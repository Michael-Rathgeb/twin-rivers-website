Do you need a permit to replace a roof in Kirkwood?

Often not, if the new roof matches the old one. Kirkwood's building code, the 2021 International Residential Code as the city adopted it (effective April 1, 2026), exempts roof covering replacement with like material, including replacing up to 25% of the roof sheathing, on roofs with a slope of 4:12 or steeper. Re-roofing a roof flatter than 4:12, or any structural change to the roof, needs a permit from the City of Kirkwood Building Division, and the city requires contractors to be licensed with it. Roofing questions from Kirkwood homeowners

What is the average cost to replace a roof on a 2,000 square foot home?

Floor area isn't roof area. A 2,000-square-foot two-story home has a smaller roof than a 2,000-square-foot ranch, and pitch and overhangs add more, so the estimate counts squares (100 square feet of roof each).

What is the 25% rule for roofing?

It's a Florida Building Code rule. St. Louis County codes have no matching percentage, so condition and age decide repair or replacement.
