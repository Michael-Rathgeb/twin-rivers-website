Roofing questions from West Alton homeowners

Does flood insurance cover roof damage?

Flood insurance covers damage from rising water. Wind and hail damage to a roof is claimed on your homeowners policy, so the two claims are separate.

Do you have to be licensed to fix a roof in Missouri?

Missouri has no statewide roofing license. Cities and counties set their own rules, so ask any roofer for proof of liability insurance.

What is the 25% rule for roofing?

It's a Florida Building Code rule. Missouri codes have no matching percentage, so condition and age decide repair or replacement.
