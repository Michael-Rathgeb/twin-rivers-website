Do you need a permit to replace a roof in St. Peters?

Not for a like-for-like re-roof, in most cases. The City of St. Peters' residential re-roofing requirements (revised March 2024) say a building permit is required when more than 25% of the sheathing needs replacing, when the roof covering changes to a different material, or when old spaced-board decking needs new panel sheathing. With or without a permit, the work must meet the code's re-roofing rules: the city's sheet calls for removing existing layers down to the sheathing unless there is a single sound layer, and requires drip edge. Roofing questions from St. Peters homeowners

What is the average cost of a new roof in Missouri?

No single average fits every house. Price follows the number of squares (100 square feet of roof each), pitch, material and decking, so get an itemized written estimate for your roof.

Do you have to be licensed to fix a roof?

Missouri has no statewide roofing license. Cities and counties set their own rules, so ask for proof of liability insurance and who pulls the permit.

Is $30,000 too much for a roof?

The roof decides, not the number. Compare estimates on the same material, number of squares and decking allowance.

What is the 25% rule for roofing?

It's a Florida Building Code rule. Missouri codes have no matching percentage, so condition and age decide repair or replacement.
