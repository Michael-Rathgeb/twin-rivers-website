Do you need a permit to replace a roof in Arnold?

Yes, in most cases. The City of Arnold requires construction permits for residential work, issued by its Community Development department's Building & Code Compliance division, and the city's permit system lists a residential roofing permit. Outside Arnold's city limits, Jefferson County Code Enforcement handles building permits for unincorporated areas, so confirm with the county whether your re-roof needs one. Price follows materials and labor more than the calendar, but late fall and winter are usually the easiest months to get on a roofer's schedule.

Do you have to be licensed to fix a roof?

Missouri has no statewide roofing license, but the City of Arnold licenses the contractors who work there. Elsewhere in Jefferson County, ask for proof of liability insurance.
