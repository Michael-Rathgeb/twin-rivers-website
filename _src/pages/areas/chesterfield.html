What Chesterfield roofs face

  • Hail: NOAA's Storm Events Database lists 162 reports of hail 1 inch or larger in St. Louis County from January 2016 through April 2026, on 36 separate days. Hail that size bruises shingles, cracks the mat and dents gutters, vents and soft metals.
  • Wind: straight-line winds lift shingle tabs and ridge caps, and once the seal breaks, the next storm finishes the job.
  • Freeze-thaw: water that gets under a lifted shingle freezes and opens the gap wider every winter.
  • Trees: mature trees in older subdivisions drop limbs on roofs and fill gutters, which pushes water back under the roof edge.

Roofing questions from Chesterfield homeowners

Will insurance cover a 20-year-old roof?

It can, if a covered storm damaged it. Many policies pay older roofs at actual cash value, which subtracts depreciation, instead of full replacement cost, and some use an age-based roof schedule. Your declarations page says which applies.

Should I tell my insurance company if I get a new roof?

Yes. A new roof can lower your premium or change how your roof is valued in a future claim, and some insurers ask for the date and material. Send them the invoice or completion paperwork.

Do you have to be licensed to fix a roof in Missouri?

Missouri has no statewide roofing license. Licensing and permits are set by each city and county, so ask a roofer for proof of liability insurance and how they handle permits where you live.

What is the best month to replace a roof?

Spring and fall give asphalt shingles the mild temperatures they need to seal. Late fall and winter are usually the easiest months to get on a roofer's schedule.

What is the 25% rule for roofing?

It's a Florida Building Code rule: repair more than 25% of a roof in 12 months and the whole roof must meet current code. St. Louis-area codes don't have a matching rule, so your roof's condition and age decide repair versus replacement.
