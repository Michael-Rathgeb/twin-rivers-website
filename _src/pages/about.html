How we work

  • We inspect before we quote. A price given from the driveway is a guess. We get on the roof, or in the attic, and photograph what we find.
  • Everything is in writing. Scope, price and warranty terms are on the estimate before you sign anything.
  • We tell you when a repair will do. Most leaks are one spot. If a roof repair will hold, we'll say so.
  • We work with your adjuster, not around them. On storm damage claims, our photos and scope go to your insurer, and we meet the adjuster on the roof.
  • We don't play games with deductibles. We never offer to pay or rebate your insurance deductible. Missouri law (RSMo 407.725) bars contractors from offering to pay or rebate any part of your insurance deductible, and a contractor who offers is asking you to mislead your insurer.
  • We clean up. The job isn't done until the yard and driveway are clear.
