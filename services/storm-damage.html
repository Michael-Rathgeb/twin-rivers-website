Hail damage roof repair

Twin Rivers Contracting LLC provides hail damage roof repair. St. Louis hail hits asphalt shingles hardest: a hailstone knocks the granules off, bruises the mat underneath and can crack it, and the shingle starts to fail from that spot. Hail damage roof repair can mean replacing shingles on one slope or, when the hits are spread across the roof, a full roof replacement your claim pays for. We also check the soft metal on the roof, such as vents, flashing and gutters, because hail dents there show how big the stones were.

One repair problem is matching. Shingle colors and styles change, so a hail damage roof repair on one slope may not match the rest of the roof. We note that in the scope so your adjuster sees it.

What does storm damage look like on a roof?

  • Hail: dark round spots where granules are gone, soft bruises you can feel, and granules collecting in the gutters.
  • Wind: shingles lifted, creased across the top or missing, with nail lines showing.
  • Metal: dents in vents, flashing, gutters and downspouts.
  • Trees and limbs: punctures, cracked decking and broken ridge lines.
  • Inside: new ceiling stains or drips after the storm.

How do you describe roof damage?

Describe what, where and when: "Missing shingles on the north slope and dented gutters on the back of the house after Tuesday's storm." Stick to what you saw and keep the photos. Don't guess at the cause; the inspection and the adjuster settle that.

How to file a hail damage roof insurance claim in Missouri

A hail damage roof insurance claim in Missouri starts with documenting the damage and reporting it to your insurer promptly. Here is how a hail damage roof insurance claim works:

  1. Get a free roof inspection to confirm hail damage.
  2. Photograph bruised or cracked shingles, dented gutters and vents.
  3. File the claim with your homeowners insurance company.
  4. Meet the insurance adjuster on the roof.
  5. Review the written scope of damage.
  6. Schedule the roof repair or replacement.

Your policy sets the filing deadline and deductible, so read it first. If you have a complaint about how a claim is handled, the Missouri Department of Commerce and Insurance (DCI) takes consumer complaints. Twin Rivers Contracting LLC documents hail damage and works with your adjuster.

How does an insurance claim work on a roof?

The adjuster prices the roof at replacement cost, subtracts depreciation for age to get actual cash value, and then subtracts your deductible. What happens to the depreciation depends on your policy. Example numbers only, not a price: a $20,000 roof, $6,000 of depreciation and a $3,000 deductible.

Is it worth going through insurance for hail damage?

It's worth it when the damage costs well more than your deductible. Many policies in hail-prone states carry a wind and hail deductible set as a percentage of dwelling coverage, often 1% or 2%. On a home insured for $300,000, that's $3,000 at 1% or $6,000 at 2%. A $4,000 repair is worth claiming on the first and not the second. Find your deductible on the declarations page before you call.

What not to say to an insurance adjuster about your roof

  • Don't guess at the cause or the date; say what you saw and when you saw it.
  • Don't say the roof "always leaked" unless it did; old leaks and storm damage are paid differently.
  • Don't agree to a number on the spot; ask for the written estimate.
  • Don't sign anything you haven't read.

Will my insurance company pay for a new roof (and will it cover a 20-year-old roof)?

Yes, if a covered storm damaged the roof badly enough to need replacing. Age alone isn't covered. On a 20-year-old roof, many policies pay actual cash value, which subtracts depreciation, and some use a roof schedule that pays less as the roof ages. That's the right column in the table above.

Missouri claim rules homeowners ask about: deadlines, deductibles, matching

  • Deadlines: your policy sets how soon you must report a loss and, on many policies, how long you have to finish repairs and collect held-back depreciation. Read the "Duties after loss" and "Loss settlement" sections.
  • Deductibles: check whether yours is a flat dollar amount or a percentage of dwelling coverage. The percentage kind is often the larger number.
  • Matching: whether the insurer pays to replace undamaged shingles that no longer match is decided by your policy wording and the adjuster's findings. Ask your insurer in writing.
  • Your right to cancel: under Section 407.725 of the Revised Statutes of Missouri, if you sign a contract for work to be paid by insurance, you can cancel it until midnight of the fifth business day after your insurer tells you in writing that all or part of the claim isn't covered.
  • The required notice: the same statute requires the contractor to give you that cancellation right in bold type, with a separate "Notice of Cancellation" form, before you sign.

How to spot a storm chaser after a St. Louis hailstorm

Storm chasers follow hail into town, sign roofs fast and leave before the warranty matters. Watch for these:

  • They knock on your door the day after the storm, with no local address.
  • Their contract for insurance-paid work has no bold cancellation notice, which Missouri law requires.
  • They offer to waive or "cover" your deductible, which Missouri law (RSMo 407.725) bars contractors from offering.
  • They want you to sign before the adjuster has seen the roof.
  • They ask for a large payment before any materials arrive.
  • They can't show proof of liability insurance.

Storm damage FAQs

How urgent is roof repair after a storm?

Urgent if water is getting in: book emergency roof tarping the same day you see it, because wet decking and insulation get worse with every rain. If nothing is leaking, get the roof inspected soon and report the claim promptly, as your policy requires.

Does a home warranty cover hail damage to a roof?

Usually not. A home warranty is a service contract for worn-out systems and appliances; hail is sudden damage, which is what homeowners insurance covers. Read the roof section of your home warranty contract to be sure.

How much does a new roof cost in Missouri?

It depends on roof size in squares, pitch, material, tear-off layers and decking. Our guide to what a new roof costs in St. Louis shows how to work out your roof's size and compare estimates.

How do I get my roof replaced for free?

The only legitimate way is a covered insurance claim, where the insurer pays for storm damage minus your deductible. You still owe the deductible. Smaller storm damage may only need repairing roof leaks, not a new roof. The small stuff left after the roof, like a ceiling patch or loose trim, is handyman work for storm touch-ups.
