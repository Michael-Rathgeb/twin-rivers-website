Storm & Hail Damage Repair

Fast response and expert guidance to restore your roof and navigate the insurance process.

When Disaster Strikes, We're Here

Severe weather can cause sudden, significant damage to your roof. Whether it's hail, high winds, fallen trees, or driving rain, the damage needs immediate attention to prevent further harm to your home's interior.

Twin Rivers Contracting LLC provides rapid response to storm damage emergencies. We'll secure your property, assess the damage, and guide you through the insurance claim process from start to finish.

24-48 Hour Response

We prioritize emergency calls and respond quickly to prevent further damage to your home.

Emergency Tarping

We'll secure exposed areas immediately to protect your home until permanent repairs can be made.

Insurance Claim Help

We document damage thoroughly and work directly with your insurance adjuster.

Complete Restoration

From temporary fixes to full repairs or replacement, we handle the entire process.

Types of Storm Damage We Repair

  • Hail Damage - Bruised, cracked, or dented shingles and metal components
  • Wind Damage - Lifted, missing, or creased shingles and damaged flashing
  • Fallen Debris - Tree limbs and branches that puncture or damage roofing
  • Water Intrusion - Leaks caused by compromised roofing materials
  • Gutter Damage - Bent, detached, or clogged gutters from storms

Our Insurance Claim Process

Dealing with insurance after storm damage can be overwhelming. We simplify the process:

  • Thorough damage inspection with detailed photo documentation
  • Written scope of damage that meets insurance requirements
  • Direct communication with your insurance adjuster
  • Assistance understanding your policy coverage
  • Fair, transparent pricing aligned with insurance estimates

We work with all major insurance companies and understand exactly what documentation they need to approve your claim.

What to Do After Storm Damage

  • Stay safe - Don't climb on a damaged roof yourself
  • Document damage - Take photos from the ground if visible
  • Call us first - We'll provide a professional assessment before you file
  • Contact your insurance - Report the damage promptly
  • Avoid "storm chasers" - Work with established local contractors
