Commercial roof systems we install

The right system depends on the building, the deck and how the roof is used. The systems we work with:

  • TPO: a single-ply thermoplastic membrane with heat-welded seams, usually white to reflect heat.
  • EPDM: a synthetic rubber membrane, common on older flat roofs across the region.
  • Modified bitumen: a multi-layer asphalt system with reinforced sheets, tough under foot traffic.
  • Metal: ribbed steel metal roofing for low-slope roofs with enough pitch, with snow guards over doors and walkways.
  • Built-up roofing: the traditional layered tar-and-gravel flat roof.
  • Roof coatings: a reflective coating over a sound existing roof to extend its life.

Repair, recover or replace a commercial roof?

Repair a commercial roof when the damage is local, recover it when the roof is dry and has only one covering, and replace it when the roof is wet or already has two layers. That last rule isn't a sales line. The International Building Code, which St. Louis-area building codes are based on, doesn't allow a recover over a roof that is water-soaked or deteriorated, or over one that already has two or more roof coverings. Wet insulation under a membrane has to come out.

Commercial roof repair and maintenance plans

Most commercial leaks start at the same few places, so a maintenance visit checks them before they fail:

  • drains, scuppers and gutters cleared and running
  • seams and laps checked for lifting
  • flashing at parapet walls, curbs and rooftop units
  • pitch pans and pipe penetrations resealed
  • ponding areas and punctures from foot traffic marked and patched
  • photos after hail or high wind for your insurance file

Smaller flat roofs on homes and mixed-use buildings are covered under roof repairs in St. Louis, and a standalone roof inspection is a good first step if you've just bought or leased a building.

How much does a commercial roof cost?

A commercial roof costs whatever its size, system and existing condition add up to, so it's priced per building after a roof inspection, not from a chart. The biggest swing is whether the old roof can stay: a recover over one dry layer skips the tear-off and disposal that a wet roof requires. You get the commercial roof cost in writing, with options when more than one makes sense.

How is a commercial roofing job estimated?

  1. Area, in squares. One square is 100 sq ft, so a 20,000 sq ft warehouse roof is 200 squares.
  2. Existing layers and wet areas, which decide tear-off versus recover.
  3. Insulation: how much is added, and whether it's tapered to move water to the drains.
  4. Edges and penetrations: linear feet of parapet and edge flashing, plus every drain, curb and pipe.
  5. Access: roof height, crane or hoist needs, and work hours that fit your tenants.

Comparing two bids? Divide each by the number of squares and check that both include the same tear-off, insulation and flashing.

The St. Louis energy standard and your roof

If your building is in the City of St. Louis and is 50,000 sq ft or larger, it falls under the City's Building Energy Performance Standard. Covered owners report energy use through ENERGY STAR Portfolio Manager and must meet a site energy use target; the first compliance deadline for most buildings was May 4, 2025, and the standard resets on four-year cycles. A roof replacement is the cheapest moment to add insulation above the deck, because the roof is open anyway. Buildings in St. Louis County aren't covered by the City's standard.

Commercial roofing FAQs

What is the 25% rule for roofing?

It's a Florida Building Code rule: repair more than 25% of a roof in 12 months and the whole roof must meet current code. Missouri codes built on the International Building Code have no matching percentage. The rules that apply here are the recover limits: no recover over a wet roof or over two existing coverings.

Is $30,000 too much for a roof?

Only the size can tell you. Divide the price by the squares: $30,000 is $600 a square on a 50-square roof and $150 a square on a 200-square roof. Then compare what each bid includes.

What is the average cost of a commercial building roof?

There isn't a useful average, because a dry recover and a full tear-off on the same building can be very different jobs. Get a per-square price on your building, with the scope in writing.

Can a commercial roof be replaced while we stay open?

Usually, yes. Flat roofs are replaced in sections, each one closed in before the crew leaves, and noisy work can be scheduled outside your busy hours.
