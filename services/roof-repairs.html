Roof repair in St. Louis, MO

Water usually gets in at one weak point, such as a cracked pipe boot, loose flashing at a wall or chimney, a valley worn thin, or a few shingles lifted by wind. Fix that point properly and the roof keeps working. As a local St. Louis roofer, we see the same handful of failures over and over, so we check those first.

Roof leak repair

Roof leak repair means tracing the water back to where it enters, which is rarely right above the stain. Water runs along decking and rafters before it drips, so we start in the attic when we can, then match what we see inside to the roof surface.

Is it normal for a roof to leak in heavy rain?

No. A sound roof doesn't leak in heavy rain. Heavy or wind-driven rain just finds the weak spots a light shower misses: flashing that has pulled away, a valley that's worn, nail holes from an old repair, or gutters so full that water backs up under the roof edge. If it only leaks in big storms, the leak is real and will get worse.

How can you stop a roof leak while it is raining?

Don't go on a wet roof. From inside, you can limit the damage:

  1. Put a bucket or tote under the drip and move furniture and electronics away.
  2. If the ceiling is bulging with water, put a bucket under it and poke a small hole at the lowest point so it drains in one place.
  3. In the attic, set a container under the drip and lay down a board so you don't step through the ceiling.
  4. Take photos of the ceiling and attic for your insurance claim.
  5. Call for emergency roof tarping if water keeps coming in.

Common roof repairs we handle

  • Flashing: resealing or replacing metal flashing at walls, chimneys and skylights, the most common leak source we find.
  • Pipe boots: rubber collars around plumbing vents crack in the sun and let water run down the pipe.
  • Missing or damaged shingles: replaced and sealed, matched as closely as the existing roof allows.
  • Valleys: where two roof slopes meet, water volume is highest and worn shingles leak first.
  • Chimneys: step and counter flashing, crickets behind wide chimneys, and failed sealant.
  • Ridge caps and vents: wind-lifted ridge caps and cracked vent housings.
  • Soffit and fascia: rotted wood behind the gutters, often from water backing up out of undersized or clogged gutters.

Flat and low-slope roof repair

Flat and low-slope roofs, common on the back of City brick homes and on small commercial buildings, leak at seams, around drains and where the roof meets a parapet wall. We repair the membrane at the failure point and check the drains, because standing water is what shortens a flat roof's life. Larger buildings are covered on our commercial flat-roof work page.

Should I repair or replace my roof?

Repair your roof when the damage is limited to one area and the rest of the roof is sound; replace your roof when damage is widespread, leaks keep returning, or the roof is near the end of its service life. A free roof inspection from Twin Rivers Contracting LLC shows which you need: we photograph the roof, explain the repair-or-replace options, and give you a written estimate for each so you can compare the cost. If replacement wins, our page on replacing your roof explains the process.

What is the 25% rule for roofing?

The "25% rule" comes from the Florida Building Code, which says that if more than 25% of a roof is repaired or replaced within 12 months, the whole roof has to be brought up to current code. It's a Florida rule. St. Louis-area codes are based on the International Residential Code, which has no matching percentage. The rule that does apply here is about layers: the residential code doesn't allow new shingles over a roof that already has two layers, or over one that's water-soaked or deteriorated. So a repair-or-replace decision here comes down to the roof's condition, its age and whether replacement shingles will still match.

How much does roof repair cost in St. Louis?

Roof repair cost in St. Louis depends on what failed and how much of the roof is affected. A small roof repair such as replacing a few shingles or a pipe boot costs less than a roof leak repair that requires new decking or flashing around a chimney. Twin Rivers Contracting LLC inspects the roof for free and gives you the roof repair cost in writing before any work begins, with no hidden fees.

What moves the roof repair cost in St. Louis:

  • How steep and how high the roof is (steeper roofs need more safety setup)
  • Whether the decking underneath is soft and has to be replaced
  • Flashing work at a chimney or wall, which takes longer than shingle work
  • Matching discontinued shingles

How much does it cost to fix a roof that only leaks in heavy rain?

Usually less than people expect, because a heavy-rain-only leak is almost always one specific spot: flashing, a valley or a pipe boot. The hard part is finding it. Once the source is found, it's normally a small roof repair, so the roof repair cost goes to the diagnosis and one fix, not a new roof.

Does homeowners insurance cover roof repair?

Homeowners insurance usually covers roof repair when the damage is sudden and accidental, such as hail, wind or a fallen tree, and usually does not cover roof repair for normal wear and age. Your policy and deductible decide what is paid. Twin Rivers Contracting LLC documents storm damage with photos, writes a scope of damage for your insurance adjuster, and completes the roof repair once the claim is approved.

When is a roof repair claim worth filing?

Do the math before you call your insurer. Many policies in hail-prone states carry a separate wind and hail deductible set as a percentage of your dwelling coverage, often 1% or 2%. On a home insured for $300,000, a 1% deductible is $3,000. If the repair costs less than that, the claim pays nothing and still goes on your record. Check the deductible on your declarations page first. For bigger storm damage, read our guide to filing a hail claim in Missouri.

Permits and licensing for roof repair in St. Louis

Missouri has no statewide roofing license, so permits and contractor licensing are set by each city and county. Small repairs generally don't need a permit, while a full replacement often does. The City of St. Louis and each St. Louis County municipality set their own rules. Ask any roofer how they handle permits where you live, and for proof of liability insurance.

Roof repair FAQs

What are the signs you need roof repair?

The signs you need roof repair are water stains on ceilings or walls, missing, cracked or curling shingles, shingle granules in the gutters, daylight through the attic boards, sagging areas on the roof, and moss or algae on the shingles. If you see any of these signs, schedule a roof repair inspection before water damages the decking, insulation and drywall. If a leak already stained a ceiling, our handyman services cover the drywall patch once the roof is fixed.

What products can I use to repair a leaking roof myself?

Roofing cement or a tube of roof sealant can slow a small leak around a nail or flashing edge, and a tarp can cover a damaged area. Both are temporary. Sealant over a failed flashing or pipe boot usually buys a season, not a fix.

How long does a roof repair take?

Most single-spot roof repairs are done in one visit once the crew is on site. A simple roof repair, such as replacing a few shingles or a pipe boot, takes less time than a roof leak repair that needs new decking or chimney flashing.

What time of year is cheapest to replace a roof?

Late fall and winter are often the slowest months for roofers, so scheduling is easier then. Asphalt shingles need mild temperatures to seal, so very cold days are avoided. Our roof replacement page covers timing and cost.
