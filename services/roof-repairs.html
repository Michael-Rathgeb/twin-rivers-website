Common Roof Repairs We Handle

  • Leak Repairs - Finding and fixing the source of water intrusion
  • Shingle Replacement - Replacing cracked, curled, or missing shingles
  • Flashing Repairs - Resealing around chimneys, vents, and skylights
  • Ridge Cap Replacement - Fixing the vulnerable peak of your roof
  • Gutter Repairs - Addressing drainage issues that affect your roof
  • Vent & Pipe Boot Repairs - Replacing deteriorated rubber seals
  • Soffit & Fascia Repairs - Fixing rotted or damaged trim boards

Signs You Need Roof Repairs

Don't ignore these warning signs that indicate your roof needs professional attention:

  • Water stains on ceilings or walls
  • Missing, cracked, or curling shingles
  • Granules accumulating in gutters
  • Daylight visible through the attic
  • Sagging areas on the roof surface
  • Higher than normal energy bills
  • Moss or algae growth on shingles
