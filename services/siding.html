Siding Installation & Repair

Protect your home and boost curb appeal with beautiful, durable siding from trusted brands.

Your Home's Protective Shell

Siding does more than make your home look great, it protects the structure from moisture, pests, and weather damage. Quality siding also provides insulation that helps control energy costs year-round.

Twin Rivers Contracting LLC installs and repairs all types of siding with meticulous attention to detail. We ensure proper installation techniques that maximize the lifespan and performance of your siding investment.

Siding Materials We Install

  • Vinyl Siding - Affordable, durable, low maintenance, wide color selection
  • Fiber Cement (Hardie Board) - Premium durability, fire resistant, authentic wood look
  • Engineered Wood - Natural appearance with improved durability over real wood
  • Insulated Vinyl - Built-in foam backing for superior energy efficiency
  • Board and Batten - Vertical style for farmhouse and modern aesthetics
  • Cedar/Wood Siding - Classic natural beauty with proper maintenance

Our Siding Services

  • Full Siding Replacement - Complete removal and new installation
  • Partial Repairs - Replace damaged sections to match existing siding
  • Re-siding Over Existing - Add new siding layer where appropriate
  • Soffit & Fascia - Complete the look with matching trim
  • Trim & Accessories - Window trim, corners, and decorative elements

Signs You Need New Siding

  • Cracked, warped, or loose panels
  • Fading color that won't improve with cleaning
  • Bubbling or blistering under the paint
  • Mold, mildew, or fungus growth
  • Rotted or soft spots (wood siding)
  • High energy bills indicating poor insulation
  • Interior water damage or peeling paint
  • Frequent need for repainting

Why Proper Installation Matters

Siding installation isn't just about nailing panels to your house. Proper installation includes:

  • Moisture barrier and house wrap installation
  • Correct overlap and fastening techniques
  • Proper clearance from ground and landscaping
  • Seamless integration around windows and doors
  • Adequate ventilation behind siding panels
