Do you need a permit to replace a roof in O'Fallon?

No. The City of O'Fallon Building Division's residential re-roofing requirements say a permit for re-covering or replacing roofing is not required, but all work must meet the adopted code. That puts the quality check on your roofer, not a city inspector, so ask for photos of the deck, underlayment and flashing before the shingles go on. If your subdivision has an HOA, check its rules on shingle color too.

Roofing questions from O'Fallon homeowners

What is the average cost of a new roof in Missouri?

There's no single figure: price follows the number of squares (100 square feet of roof each), pitch, material and decking. An itemized written estimate for your roof is the reliable number.

Do you have to be licensed to fix a roof?

Missouri has no statewide roofing license. Cities and counties set their own rules, so ask any roofer for proof of liability insurance and how they handle permits in O'Fallon.

Is $30,000 too much for a roof?

The roof decides, not the number. A large, steep roof with a second layer to tear off costs far more than a small, simple one, so compare estimates on the same material and number of squares.

What is the 25% rule for roofing?

It's a Florida Building Code rule. IRC-based codes around St. Louis have no matching percentage, so condition and age decide repair or replacement.
