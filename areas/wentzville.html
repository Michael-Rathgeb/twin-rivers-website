Hail bruising across several slopesReplacement, often through a claim

The last row is a code rule, not a preference: the International Residential Code, which local codes here are built on, doesn't allow a new layer over two existing layers or over a water-soaked or deteriorated roof.

Do you need a permit to replace a roof in Wentzville?

Roof replacement permit rules in Wentzville, MO are set locally, because Missouri has no statewide roofing license or statewide roofing permit system. The City of Wentzville's Building Division issues permits through its online portal and requires contractors to hold a city-issued contractor license, but its published permit list doesn't single out re-roofing. Ask the city before you replace your roof, ask your roofer for their Wentzville license, and check your HOA's rules on shingle color.

Roofing questions from Wentzville homeowners

How do you find a good roofing company?

Ask for a certificate of liability insurance, a written and itemized estimate, photos of what they found and who pulls the permit. Be wary of anyone asking for full payment up front.

Do you have to be licensed to fix a roof?

Missouri has no statewide roofing license. Cities and counties set their own rules, so ask how the roofer handles permits in Wentzville.

Is $30,000 too much for a roof?

It depends on the squares, pitch, material and decking. Compare estimates on the same material and the same number of squares.

What is the 25% rule for roofing?

It's a Florida Building Code rule. Missouri has no matching percentage, so condition and age decide repair or replacement.
