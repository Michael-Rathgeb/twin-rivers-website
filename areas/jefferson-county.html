What Arnold roofs face: storms, hail and freeze-thaw

  • Hail, on the record: NOAA's Storm Events Database lists 68 reports of hail 1 inch or larger in Jefferson County from January 2016 through April 2026, on 30 separate days. The largest was 3 inches near Hillsboro on November 5, 2017.
  • One bad day: on March 14, 2025, the county logged 23 of those reports, including 1.75-inch hail near Imperial and 1-inch hail near Arnold.
  • Wind and trees: wooded lots and ridgelines catch straight-line winds, and falling limbs puncture shingles and decking.
  • Freeze-thaw: water under a lifted shingle freezes and widens the gap every winter.

Do you need a permit to replace a roof in Arnold?

Yes, in most cases. The City of Arnold requires construction permits for residential work, issued by its Community Development department's Building & Code Compliance division, and the city's permit system lists a residential roofing permit. The city also says only contractors licensed by the City of Arnold may work there, and it has adopted the 2021 International Residential Code. Outside Arnold's city limits, Jefferson County Code Enforcement handles building permits for unincorporated areas, so confirm with the county whether your re-roof needs one.

Roofing questions from Arnold homeowners

What is the average cost of a roof repair?

It depends on what failed and how far water has spread: resealing a pipe boot is a small job, while rebuilding a chimney flashing or replacing rotted decking is a larger one. You get the price in writing first.

Is it cheaper to replace a roof with shingles or metal?

Asphalt shingles cost less to install. Metal costs more up front and is chosen for its longer service life.

What time of year is the cheapest to replace a roof?

Price follows materials and labor more than the calendar, but late fall and winter are usually the easiest months to get on a roofer's schedule.

Do you have to be licensed to fix a roof?

Missouri has no statewide roofing license, but the City of Arnold licenses the contractors who work there. Elsewhere in Jefferson County, ask for proof of liability insurance.
