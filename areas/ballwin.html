Do you need a permit to replace a roof in Ballwin?

Roof replacement permit rules in Ballwin, MO are set locally, because Missouri has no statewide roofing license or statewide roofing permit system. In St. Louis County, some cities run their own building departments and others contract with the county's Department of Transportation and Public Works, and a 2021 St. Louis REALTORS® white paper counted no fewer than 42 building codebooks in use across the county. Roofing questions from Ballwin homeowners

What is the average cost of a new roof in Missouri?

No single average fits every house. Price follows the number of squares (100 square feet of roof each), the pitch, the material and the decking, so get an itemized written estimate for your roof.

Do you have to be licensed to fix a roof?

Missouri has no statewide roofing license. Cities and counties set their own rules, so ask for proof of liability insurance and who pulls the permit.

Is $30,000 too much for a roof?

Only the roof can answer that. Compare estimates on the same material, the same number of squares and the same decking allowance before you call a price high.

What is the 25% rule for roofing?

It's a Florida Building Code rule. IRC-based codes in St. Louis County have no matching percentage, so condition and age decide repair or replacement.
